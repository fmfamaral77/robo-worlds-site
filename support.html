Questions, bugs or feedback: fmfamaral@gmail.com. I reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Drag left and right to steer. Tap to jump, and tap again in the air for a double jump. Jump, then hold your finger down to glide, and swipe down to slam into the ground; tap just as you land for a mega bounce. The first level teaches each move as you go.

How do I open more levels?

Reach the golden gate at the end of a level to open the next one. Worlds 1 and 2 and Endless mode are free. The Full Game, a one-time purchase, opens Worlds 3 to 5 and two extra robot skins.

How do I get new robot skins?

Collect golden stars as you run. Your lifetime total unlocks new skins, which you pick with the SKINS button on the title screen. Two skins come with the Full Game.

Why does the game ask a multiplication question?

That is the grown-up check. Buying or restoring the Full Game first asks a multiplication question written in words, so children cannot make a purchase by accident.

I bought the Full Game. How do I get it on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings (the gear on the title screen) and choose Restore purchase.

The levels are too hard for my child

Turn on Easy Mode in Settings. Platforms become wider and longer, and the gaps between them shorter.

How do I start over?

Choose Settings > Reset progress, then tap it again to confirm. This clears levels, stars and records. A purchased Full Game stays unlocked.

Sound and vibration

Settings has separate switches for sound and haptics.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline, with no ads and no accounts. Buying or restoring the Full Game needs a connection to the App Store.
