Robo Worlds Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Robo Worlds is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Fernando Amaral for children and families. This policy explains what the app does with information about you and your child.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising or analytics code, and it works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, haptics and Easy Mode) and your progress (levels opened, stars collected, best score and chosen robot skin) are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your device backups like any app data. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The app is free to download and offers one optional in-app purchase, the Full Game. Buying or restoring it first asks a grown-up question, so a child cannot buy by accident. Apple takes the payment: I never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Whether you own it is checked with Apple on your device, and nothing about it is sent to me.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage data with me. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Robo Worlds is made for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and it has no ads, no social features and no links out of the app.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
